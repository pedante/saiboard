## Mandatory verification for CHG-007 and CHG-008

The counterpart zones stay priority0: In1 GND `47f45fd7-05d8-4264-b78d-4a4cce2ee78b`, In2 P `2f5d64fb-0afd-415d-874d-0afc87e27243`. Each recommended action is **a zone-setting change plus refill**, not a hand edit of a polygon. It may still change the resulting copper. Expected result: its one equal-priority intersection disappears; after both, the two reported zone errors should be zero. This is an expectation, not a claim of a completed DRC.

After individual approval, preserve a fresh pre-change copy and process one CHG at a time. Use identical tool version, plot options and origin to produce before/after audit Gerbers. Compare parsed tracks, pads, vias, nets, layers, zone boundaries/settings, outline and holes; only the approved priority and resulting fill may differ. For every copper layer compare normalized vector geometry by symmetric difference (XOR), reporting differing area and bounding boxes, thermal/spoke changes, islands and connections. No unrelated copper difference is acceptable without another approval. Rerun ERC, DRC and schematic/PCB pin membership checks after each change. Any unexpected difference means stop and retain/revert the isolated candidate, not silently expand the fix.

In Phase6 also compare historical outline, drill count/positions and copper geometry. Historical electrical revision remains UNVERIFIED: it is a third reference, not authority to force new copper back to it. Gerber file byte equality alone is inadequate because timestamps/order may differ. Neither refilling nor new plotting was performed during this Phase3 analysis.
