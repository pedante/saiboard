General Description                                            Features

The AH49E is a small, versatile linear Hall-effect             ·   Miniature Construction
device that is operated by the magnetic field from a           ·   Power Consumption of 3.5mA at VCC=5V for
permanent magnet or an electromagnet. The output                   Energy Efficiency
voltage is set by the supply voltage and varies in pro-
                                                               ·   Single Current Sourcing Output
portion to the strength of the magnetic field.
                                                               ·   Linear Output for Circuit Design Flexibility
The integrated circuitry features low noise output,            ·   Low Noise Output Virtually Eliminates the Need
which makes it unnecessary to use external filtering. It           for Filtering
also includes precision resistors to provide increased         ·   A Stable and Accurate Output

industrial applications.
